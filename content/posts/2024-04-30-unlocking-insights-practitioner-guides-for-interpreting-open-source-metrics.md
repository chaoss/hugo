I am thrilled to announce that we have just launched a series of [Practitioner Guides](https://github.com/chaoss/wg-data-science/tree/main/practitioner-guides#contributions) to help people develop meaningful open source project health insights.

Today, we have released the first four guides in the series:

- Introduction – Things to Think about When Interpreting Metrics (Please start here)
- Responsiveness
- Contributor Sustainability
- Organizational Participation

These guides are designed for practitioners who may or may not be experts in data analysis or open source. The goal is to help people understand how to interpret data about an open source project and develop insights that improve project health.

The Practitioner Guides are for Open Source Program Offices (OSPOs), project leads, community managers, maintainers, and anyone who wants to better understand project health and take action on their metrics.

Each guide explains how to identify trends, diagnose issues, gather additional data, make improvements, and monitor results.

We welcome your contributions — you can propose new guides, improve existing ones, or submit pull requests.

These guides are developed within the CHAOSS Data Science Working Group.
